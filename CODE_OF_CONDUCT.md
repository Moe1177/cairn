# Code of conduct

cairn adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:
- Be welcoming, respectful, and patient.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- No publishing other people's private information.
- No sexualised language or imagery.

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when you represent
the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers. Use the repository's **Security** tab
(**Report a vulnerability**, which is private), or contact a maintainer directly through GitHub.
All reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions that don't follow this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behaviour they judge harmful. Enforcement follows
the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines).
